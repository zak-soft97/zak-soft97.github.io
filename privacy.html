Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Some of our applications may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. Some apps may use services such as Firebase or other third-party tools for authentication, cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting or app functionality. The services used by a particular app will be described in that app’s disclosures where applicable.

How we use information

We may use information to provide and maintain app features, authenticate users, prevent abuse, diagnose technical problems, improve our products and comply with legal obligations.

Data retention and deletion

Retention depends on the purpose for which information is collected and the service involved. If an app provides an account or user data, deletion options will be described within the app or its dedicated privacy documentation.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can review the matter.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information on our Contact page.
